Bundled OCR models
==================

PP-OCRv6_small_det.onnx       PP-OCRv6 small text detection (DBNet)
PP-OCRv6_small_rec.onnx       PP-OCRv6 small text recognition (CTC)
PP-OCRv6_small_rec_dict.txt   the recognition model's character dictionary (18708 entries)

These are the PaddleOCR PP-OCRv6 small inference models as exported to ONNX by the
PaddlePaddle authors and published on Hugging Face:

  https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_det_onnx   (inference.onnx)
    SHA-256 d73e0058b7a8086bbd57f3d10b8bcd4ff95363f67e06e2762b5e814fe9c9410e
  https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_rec_onnx   (inference.onnx)
    SHA-256 5435fd747c9e0efe15a96d0b378d5bd157e9492ed8fd80edf08f30d02fa24634

The dictionary is the `PostProcess.character_dict` list of that repository's
inference.yml, one entry per line; the ONNX export carries no `character` metadata.

They are used by the refinery extractor as a classical-OCR cross-reader (a decorrelated
second opinion on the numeric cells the VLM mis-reads — see TextDetector.kt / DigitOcr.kt /
PanelOcr.kt).
